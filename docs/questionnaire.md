Publish this as a Google Form. Email collection should stay optional because the brief is sensitive.

**Form title:** IRO — Issues around AAUA and Akungba communities

**Description:** For AAUA students and indigenes of Akungba, Iwaro, Etioro, and Ayegunle. Do not put your house address or phone number.

## Section 1 — Who you are

1. I am mainly a… (required) — AAUA student / Indigene or resident / Both / Other
2. Which place do you know best? (required) — AAUA campus or hostels / Akungba / Iwaro / Etioro / Ayegunle / More than one
3. How long have you lived or studied here? — Under 1 year / 1–3 years / More than 3 years

## Section 2 — The problems

4. Which issues should IRO accept? (required checkboxes) — Security, Water, Power, Roads and flood, Hostel, Harassment, Health, Waste, Other
5. In the last month, which of these affected you? (same list)
6. Where do people usually take a complaint today? — Student affairs or SUG / Hostel porter / Security post / Community leader / WhatsApp only / Nowhere
7. What stops people from reporting? — Fear of being identified / Nothing happens / Do not know who to tell / Language / No light or data / Office is far

## Section 3 — What the outlet should do

8. A report should be able to stay anonymous. (scale 1–5)
9. How should a reporter hear back? — Reference number on the page / WhatsApp / Email / No follow-up
10. Which status words are clear? — Open / In review / Resolved / Rejected
11. Anything the outlet must not ask for? (optional paragraph)
12. One issue you would report this week, with the area but not your house number. (optional)
