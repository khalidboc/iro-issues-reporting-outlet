About IRO

IRO (Issues Reporting Outlet) is a mini web platform designed to help AAUA students and indigenes of Akungba, Iwaro, Etioro, Ayegunle and nearby communities report issues affecting them in a structured and accessible way.

The goal is to bridge the gap between community members and relevant authorities by providing a simple digital channel for documenting problems such as security, infrastructure, utilities, environment, academic facilities and more.
